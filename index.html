About Us

A Family Firm That Does the Work Itself

We are a family-run firm, and the brickwork and carpentry on your job is done by us, not handed off. For everything else we bring in electricians, plumbers, roofers and plasterers we have worked with for years, and we manage the whole lot so it runs in the right order.

One point of contact from the first visit to the final snag. We organise every trade on your job, so you are never left chasing five different firms.
